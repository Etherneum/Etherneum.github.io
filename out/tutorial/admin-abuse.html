Admin Abuse

A weekly Sunday update event with free items, Astronaut mutation, lobby obby rewards, and boss spawn loot.

When it starts

Admin Abuse starts at 6 AM PST on Sundays, which is typically when a new update drops. In other time zones, that is usually around 9 AM EST / 2 PM UTC.

✨

Free items

Collect free traits, essence, and other resources during the event window.

🚀

Astronaut mutation

Astronaut is active for Admin Abuse, making it the best mutation to use.

🎯

Obby at the Lobby

Complete the lobby obby for additional rewards and bonus loot.

👾

Boss spawn

A boss appears during Admin Abuse. Defeat it to earn extra rewards.

How to make the most of it

  • Gear up before Sunday

    Have a strong team ready ahead of time so you can clear event content quickly once the update drops.

  • Hit the lobby obby

    Complete the Obby at the Lobby for bonus rewards on top of the normal event drops.

  • Focus the boss

    Defeating the boss during Admin Abuse gives extra loot, so bring your best units.
